PROXENIX OSCC EARLY TRIAGE PROTOCOL DISCLOSURE

This screening tool uses AI-assisted optical image analysis to help identify visual patterns that may be associated with early-stage oral squamous cell carcinoma (OSCC) risk indicators.

The analysis method combines convolutional feature extraction with a clinical decision threshold to produce a triage classification: Normal, Suspicious, or High Risk. This classification is a screening aid only.

Clinical Disclaimer: This tool does not provide a medical diagnosis. It does not replace evaluation by a licensed healthcare professional, histopathological biopsy, or any standard-of-care diagnostic protocol. All results, regardless of classification, should be interpreted by qualified clinical staff.

Data Privacy: Captured images and screening results are stored locally within this browser session for record-keeping and report generation. No data is transmitted externally unless Live API mode is explicitly enabled by clinical staff in Portal Settings.

Patient Consent: By proceeding, the patient (or their authorized representative) acknowledges understanding of the screening purpose, its limitations, and voluntarily consents to the capture and AI-assisted analysis of an oral cavity image for triage purposes.

A qualified clinician must review all results and determine appropriate next steps, including referral for professional medical diagnosis where indicated.

Clinical Guidelines

Clinician Visual Exam Steps

  • Systematically inspect the lips for texture and color changes
  • Examine buccal mucosa on both sides for patches or lesions
  • Check gingiva for swelling, bleeding, or discoloration
  • Inspect the dorsal, ventral, and lateral tongue surfaces
  • Palpate and visually examine the floor of the mouth
  • Review the hard palate for irregularities
  • Review the soft palate and uvula
  • Visualize the oropharynx where accessible

High-Risk Sign Profiles

  • Erythroplakia — red patches that cannot be attributed to other causes
  • Non-Healing Ulcers — persisting beyond 2-3 weeks
  • Leukoplakia — white patches that cannot be scraped off
  • Oral Submucous Fibrosis — progressive stiffening of oral tissue

Supportive Nutrition & Diet

  • Emphasize antioxidant-rich vegetables and fruits
  • Encourage Vitamin B, C, and zinc-rich foods
  • Maintain consistent hydration throughout the day
  • Avoid tobacco, alcohol, and highly processed foods

Clinical Hygiene Protocol

  • Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste for 2 minutes
  • Use antiseptic mouthwash as clinically indicated
  • Schedule professional cleaning every 6 months
  • Maintain denture hygiene with daily cleaning routines

These guidelines are educational references for clinical staff and do not replace professional diagnostic protocols or local institutional policy.
